INCIDENT SNAPSHOT

What happened?

CONFIRMED FACTS

On 29 April 2021, an attacker used an employee username and password through a legacy VPN profile that did not require a one-time passcode. Colonial detected the ransomware incident on 7 May and shut down pipeline operations as a precaution while containing the threat.

Initial access identified29 Apr 2021
Ransomware discovered7 May 2021
Pipeline restart announced13 May 2021
KEY RISK MANAGEMENT FINDING

Cyber risk became
operational risk.

The attack affected business IT. The company halted pipeline operations to reduce the risk of further compromise and protect operational technology.

06 / INCIDENT SEQUENCE

Key events

Dates documented in official statements and the supplied case study.

29 APR 2021Initial access

Legacy VPN profile accessed using employee credentials; no one-time passcode required.

7 MAY 2021Ransomware discovered

DarkSide ransomware identified; pipeline operations proactively shut down.

8 MAY 2021Ransom payment

Approximately 75 BTC, reported at about $4.3–$4.4 million, paid.

10 MAY 2021FBI attribution

FBI confirmed DarkSide was responsible for the network compromise.

13 MAY 2021Pipeline restart

Colonial announced the entire pipeline system had restarted.

7 JUN 2021DOJ seizure

DOJ announced seizure of 63.7 BTC valued at approximately $2.3 million.
